## A Vision at Twilight 

I was standing in a park at twilight. The sky was a little cloudy. Nearby, my parents and a few family friends were standing and talking, while some others were walking around the park.

Suddenly, I noticed a brightness in the sky. When I looked up, I saw the clouds were slowly clearing. Through the gap, I could see stars - but they weren't the usual silvery-white stars. These were golden and shining, some of them colorful and bright. All the stars seemed to be moving and sparkling, almost like they were alive.

I was amazed and confused at the same time. I turned to the people near me and asked if they could see the golden, colorful stars moving and sparkling in the sky. But they said no - to them, it just looked cloudy, nothing unusual.

I kept watching anyway, enjoying this beautiful sight. Then, near the 10 o'clock direction in the sky, I saw a bright white light shining among the stars. I looked closely, and I saw it was a glowing white horse - so beautiful. When I looked at the one riding the horse, I saw it was Jesus. He wore a glowing white robe with a soft red sash across it. His hair was a mild golden color, shining and bright, and he wore a beautiful crown.

He rode the horse gently through the clouds, moving past me toward the 8 o'clock direction in the sky. That direction pointed exactly toward the new house my family and I have been thinking about buying.

Then, from the 2 o'clock direction, I saw a few angels flying through the sky, also heading toward the same house, following behind Jesus and the white horse.
I felt so happy and overwhelmed with joy. I excitedly told my family and friends nearby, "Do you see that? They're all flying toward our new house!" My heart was full of happiness, and I woke up feeling calm and peaceful.
